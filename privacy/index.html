Two-Faced — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Two-Faced is a party game for Android made by an independent developer in India ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects and why. The short version: no accounts, no ads, no tracking companies, and nothing that tells us who you are.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No advertising ID. The app shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code.

What the app keeps on your phone

Your player name, your game record, your settings, downloaded characters and the data needed to replay or re-make a movie of your games. This stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What is sent to our server

1. A random phone code. On first start the app makes up a random code. It is not your phone's serial number or any Google ID. Our server uses it to recognise the same phone between games. For usage counts the server stores only a scrambled (hashed) version of it.

2. Online games. When you play or watch online, our server receives your player name, your character and your moves. It keeps a record of finished online games, including the player names and moves, so games can be watched and replayed, and it keeps a count of betrayals linked to the random phone code. Do not use your real name as your player name if you don't want it shown to other players.

3. Usage counts. To learn whether the game works for players, the app sends simple counts: app opened, games started, finished or left, rounds reached, truces and betrayals used, store opened, character chosen, movie made or shared. These contain no names, no message contents and no internet address. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy → Share usage stats. When it is off, nothing is sent and anything waiting to be sent is deleted.

Our server is in a data centre run by Oracle Cloud. All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). We do not sell or share this data with anyone, and we do not use it to identify you.

Shared movies and replays

You can make a movie of a game and share it through your phone's share menu. Movies show the player names used in that game. You choose whether and where to share a movie; we do not post anything for you.

How long we keep data

Usage counts and online game records are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted. A phone's betrayal count is deleted 12 months after it last changed.

Your choices

  • Switch off usage counts in Settings → Privacy.
  • Play bot games only: they never send game data to our server (only the usage counts above, if switched on).
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your phone.
  • Because we do not know who you are, we cannot look up your data by name or email. If you want online records deleted, write to us and we will help as far as we can.

Children

Two-Faced is meant for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
